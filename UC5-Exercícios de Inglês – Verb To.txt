UC5-Exercícios de Inglês – Verb To Be / Do, Does
Nível Básico:

1. Complete com am / is / are
a) I AM_ a student.
b) She IS_ my friend.
c) They ARE_ happy.
d) We ARE_ in the classroom.
e) He IS_ a teacher.

2. Complete com do / does
a) DO_ you like pizza?
b) DOES_ she play soccer?
c) DO_ they study English?
d) DOES_ he work here?
e) DO_ we have class today?

3. Complete com o verbo correto (Simple Present)
a) I STUDY_ (study) every day.
b) She LIKES_ (like) chocolate.
c) They PLAY_ (play) soccer.
d) He WORKS_ (work) a lot.
e) We LIVE_ (live) in Brazil.

4. Transforme em negativa
a) I am tired. → __I am not tired.___
b) She is my sister. → __She isn't my sister.___
c) They are at home. → __They aren't at home.___
d) You like coffee. → __You don't like coffee.__
e) He plays football. → __He doesn't play football.___

Nível Médio
5. Transforme em interrogativa
a) You are happy. → __Are you happy?___
b) She is at school. → __Is she at school?___
c) They are friends. → __Are they friends?___
d) You like music. → __Do you like music?___
e) He works a lot. → __Does he work a lot?___

6. Complete com do / does / did
a) Did_ you go to the party yesterday?
b) Does_ she like chocolate?
c) Did_ they finish the homework?
d) Does_ he study every day?
e) Did_ you watch the movie last night?

7. Complete com was / were
a) I was_ at home yesterday.
b) They were_ very tired.
c) She was_ at the party.
d) We were_ late.
e) He was_ sick last week.

8. Coloque na forma negativa (Simple Present)
a) She likes pizza. → __She does not like pizza.__
b) He works here. → __He do not work here.__
c) They study English. → __They do not study in English.__
d) You play soccer. → __you do not play soccer.__
e) We watch TV. → __We do not watch TV.__

9. Corrija as frases
a) She do her homework every day. → _She does her homework every day.
b) They is very happy. → _They are very happy.
c) He don't like pizza. → _He does't like pizza.
d) I is a teacher. → _I am a teacher.
e) We does not understand. → _We do not understand.

Nível Difícil
10. Complete corretamente
a) She was_ (be) at home yesterday.
b) They did_ (do) their homework last night.
c) He is_ (be) very happy today.
d) We do_ (do) not understand the exercise.
e) I was_ (be) tired last week.

11. Reescreva no passado
a) I am tired. → _I was tired._
b) She is at school. → _She was at school._
c) They do homework. → _They did homework._
d) He does the work. → _He did the work._
e) We are friends. → _We were friends._

12. Complete com a forma correta (Simple Present)
a) She goes_ (go) to school every day.
b) He have_ (have) a car.
c) They eat_ (eat) lunch at noon.
d) It rains_ (rain) a lot here.
e) We study_ (study) English on Mondays.

13. Complete com a forma correta
a) Do_ you like_ (like) pizza?
b) Does_ she goes_ (go) to school yesterday?
c) They do_ not understand_ (understand).
d) He does_ not study_ (study) yesterday.
e) Do_ they were_ (be) at home last night?

14. Traduza
a) Você estuda todos os dias. → _You study every day.__
b) Ela trabalha muito. → _She works a lot.__
c) Eles gostam de música. → _They like music.__
d) Você joga futebol? → _Do you play soccer?__
e) Nós não assistimos TV. → _We don't watch TV.__

15. Crie frases
Use as palavras para formar frases corretas:

a) (she / like / coffee) → _Does she like coffee?_
b) (they / study / English) → _They don't study English._
c) (he / not / work / here) → _He does not work here with us._
d) (you / play / soccer) → _Do you play soccer?_
e) (we / watch / TV / every day) → _We watch TV every day, if is that your question._
